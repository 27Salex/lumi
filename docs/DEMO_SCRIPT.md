# Demo video script (~90 s)

A short screen recording for the README and social posts. Record on the phone (Android's screen recorder with
microphone audio) in English, light theme, with a few realistic tasks already in place. Keep each scene under 15 s.

| # | Scene | What to do / say | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home | Open Lumi. Pause on the greeting, the number of tasks for today and Lumi's summary. | The daily brief, written on the phone |
| 2 | Natural language | Tap the bar and say: "Remind me to call the bank tomorrow at 5, it's urgent." | Date, time and priority understood; a clean title |
| 3 | Follow-up | Say: "Actually, move it to Friday." | Conversation context: "it" = the last task |
| 4 | Several things at once | "Add milk, bread and coffee, and set a timer for 10 minutes." | Brain dump + a phone action in one sentence |
| 5 | Place reminder | "Let Mum know when I get home." Open the task: show the linked contact and "Try now". | Place reminders with a ready-to-send message |
| 6 | A question | "What can I cook tonight with eggs and potatoes?" | General questions answered on-device by Gemma (not turned into a task) |
| 7 | Weather | "Will it rain this afternoon?" | Open-Meteo forecast phrased by code, no invented numbers |
| 8 | Plan | "What should I do now?" | Day plan around calendar, work hours and priorities |
| 9 | From anywhere | Lock the phone, long-press the side button (Lumi as the digital assistant): "Good night." | Lock screen pill + routine: smart alarm, Do Not Disturb, tomorrow's summary |
| 10 | Spanish | Switch to Spanish and say: «recuérdame regar las plantas cuando llegue a casa». | Bilingual: the reply follows the language you speak |
| 11 | Privacy card | End on Settings → Intelligence showing "Gemma · on device". Text overlay: "Runs on your phone. Open source." | Private by design |

Tips:
- Turn on "Spoken replies" for scenes 2–9 so the voice answers are part of the video.
- Gemma answers take a few seconds on the phone; cut the waiting in editing, but keep one real wait (scene 6) to be honest.
- Hide personal data: use demo contacts and tasks, and blur notification content in scene 9.
